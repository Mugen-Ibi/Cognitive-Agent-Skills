# Standard protocol

Use for substantive, revisable decisions and multi-step work with interacting constraints or meaningful alternatives.

## Sequence

1. **Intent** — establish outcome, constraints, decision owner, success criteria, and known context.
2. **Discover** — look for hidden dependencies, stakeholders, assumptions, adjacent concepts, second-order effects, and information gaps that could change the result.
3. **Reframe** — when the framing affects the result, construct two to four plausible formulations and choose the one with the greatest decision value. Otherwise record the compact framing check and continue.
4. **Explore** — develop materially different solution models when real alternatives exist; compare benefit, cost, complexity, reversibility, dependencies, and risk.
5. **Attack** — apply a challenge proportional to the decision. Assume the leading proposal is wrong and test the counterexamples, failure scenarios, incentive problems, boundary conditions, or alternative causal explanations most likely to change it.
6. **Verify** — verify decision-changing claims; separate facts, inferences, assumptions, and unresolved uncertainty.
7. **Compress** — reduce the explored space to one recommendation and at most two strong alternatives.
8. **Decide** — leave consequential values and risk acceptance to the user; choose delegated, reversible implementation details directly.
9. **Execute** — translate an authorized decision into artifacts, actions, and acceptance checks; perform and test them when possible.
10. **Audit** — confirm goal satisfaction, factual support, artifact quality, and whether new evidence invalidated an earlier assumption.

This terminal audit fulfills the shared completion gate; do not add a second audit. Classify unresolved decision-changing uncertainty proportionally:

- If it could reverse the recommendation or make implementation unsafe or unusable, mark the result provisional and resolve it, escalate, or return the exact blocker.
- If the decision is reversible or conditional within the user's stated risk tolerance, expose the uncertainty, contingency, and switch condition and allow a conditional recommendation.
- If action is time-bound and uncertainty is inherently irreducible, present bounded options and contingencies and reserve residual-risk acceptance for the user.

## Default decision brief

- Recommendation
- Why it leads
- Strongest alternatives
- Main trade-offs
- Largest uncertainty
- Switch condition
- Next action

Keep rejected branches available for expansion without dumping them into the initial response.
