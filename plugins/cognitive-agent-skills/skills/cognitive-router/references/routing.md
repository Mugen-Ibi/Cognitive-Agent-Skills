# Ambiguous depth choices

These examples calibrate effort; they are not a scoring algorithm or a mandatory state machine.

| Situation | Useful depth |
|---|---|
| Disposable names, layouts, or exploratory questions | Lite |
| Revisable architecture, purchase comparison, or research question selection | Standard |
| Production security approval, adoption of durable governance, or final research methodology | High Precision |

Judge the deliverable's intended reliance, not topic keywords. Brainstorming research questions is different from certifying a method for publication. A request to be thorough does not by itself make a reversible choice consequential.

For coupled decisions, preserve the rigor needed by their consequential dependencies. For separable work, vary effort locally without formal transitions or repeated audits. A downstream artifact may remain provisional while earlier work is complete.

Urgency changes the affordable investigation, not the truth of unverified claims. If time prevents closure, give a conditional recommendation with the unresolved risk visible. Do not imply that this skill changes the host model or reasoning-effort setting.
